Sonic Frontiers is the most recent game continuation of the Sonic The Hedgehog timeline. This game follows Sonic and his friends journeying to and being trapped on the Starfall Islands. These islands were the final home of the Ancients, a group of aliens who wielded the Chaos Emeralds alongside advanced technology, before they were hunted and wiped out by The End. The souls of the Ancients reside in small creatures called Koco and it’s up to Sonic and his friends to help them relive their final moments in life so their spirits can move on. In addition, Sonic has to find a way to free his friends from the cyberspace the Ancients developed to contain The End. This game holds themes of letting go of the past, managing the concept of death, and what to do in the time we have before death to make life meaningful.
